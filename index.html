05Usability analysis

Written from the point of view of a shopper who wants to get from "I like this brand" to "I bought it and I'm earning points" with as little effort as possible. Steps follow the order a shopper meets them. Each point says what slows the shopper down and a concrete fix.

1. Arrive

  • What works. shopmitten.com opens straight onto products: no age gate, cookie banner or newsletter pop-up. The announcement bar offers 15% off with code Mitten15 and free shipping over $99, which is useful to know before browsing.
  • Announcement link rotates away. The "Elevate Your Inbox" slide moves on about every 8 seconds and does not pause on hover, so a shopper reaching for it often misses it. Fix: pause the slider on hover and keyboard focus, or show one static message.
  • Reward button in the way. On smaller desktop windows (about 1024 px wide) the floating "Reward" launcher sits on top of hero text, and it covers product cards as the page scrolls (M-10). Fix: shrink it to an icon and move it clear of content.
  • Mitten Club opens on a logo. The first screen of mittenclub.shopmitten.com is only the logo. A visitor has to scroll to learn what the club is. Fix: one line on the first screen ("Earn points on every Mitten product you buy, redeem for merch") and a Join button.

2. Understand

  • The header asks you to "Validate", not to join. A newcomer does not know what validating means, and in the crawl clicking it did nothing on desktop or mobile (H-7). Fix: header items "Join" and "Verify a product", with Verify linked to /verify.
  • Two names for one action. The header says "Validate", the page says "VERIFY PRODUCT". Fix: pick one word and use it everywhere, including emails.
  • How to redeem is never explained. The rewards page lists ways to earn (complete profile 300, app 200, scan campaigns 25-50, referral) but nothing says what points buy or how. Fix: a short "How it works: earn, then redeem" block on the home page and the rewards page, with the reward catalogue.

3. Find

  • No search. There is no search box in the shop header, and /search shows a "LOREM IPSUM" placeholder with no results and no empty state (H-1). A shopper who knows what they want has to browse every collection. Fix: turn on header search with predictive results, build the results template, and add a "no results" message with popular products.
  • No sort. The In Stock filter works, but there is no sort control and sort_by in the URL is ignored: $5, $10 and $40 items appear before $3 keychains when asking for price low to high (H-2). A price-conscious shopper has to scan the whole grid. Fix: add a sort menu (featured, price low-high, price high-low, newest) wired to Shopify sort_by.
  • Navigation works. Hoodies and Jackets open the right collections.
  • Dispensary finder is one long list. 498 stores with no search or filter, the tab title says "ShopMitten" and each address repeats the store name. Finding the closest store means scrolling. Fix: a city/ZIP search and "near me", a proper title, and addresses without the duplicated name.
  • Dead clicks in the store finder. Store names in the home page list look clickable but do nothing (L-2), and on a store page SHOP NOW shows "Sorry! Cannot process your request. Try again" (H-8). Fix: link the names to the store page, and point SHOP NOW at the store's menu or at shopmitten.com, or hide it.

4. Choose

  • Sold-out sizes are unlabeled. On Classic 1 Hoodie Grey, S/M/L are only struck through. Some shoppers will not read a strike-through as "sold out" and will click it. Fix: add "Sold out" text or a tooltip, and a "notify me" option.
  • Size guide is incomplete. It is cm/kg only and leaves out 3XL. A US shopper has to convert units and still cannot check the largest size. Fix: inches/lbs alongside cm/kg, and every size listed.
  • Copy does not match the product. The description mentions "design 3" and "black, white, and grey" on a grey-only product, which makes shoppers wonder whether they are ordering the right thing. Fix: rewrite the description for this product.
  • Broken label in the footer. A social link reads "Translation missing: en.general.social.links.weedmaps". Small, but it looks unfinished. Fix: add the missing locale key.

5. Cart

  • What works. The cart drawer opens on add; quantity 2 shows $100.00; removing empties the cart; totals are right.
  • The cart's discount box does nothing. Apply in the cart or drawer does not check the code and does not carry it to checkout ("Code is applied at checkout"), so the shopper types it twice and only finds out at checkout whether it works. Fix: apply the code to the cart through Shopify's discount handling and show the saving there, or remove the field from the cart.
  • Black band on the cart page. A thick empty band sits above "YOUR CART", which pushes the cart down. Fix: remove the empty section.

6. Checkout

  • What works. Guest checkout without a phone number, clear shipping choices (Economy $7.50, 5-8 days; Standard $10.50, 3-4 days), a transparent total ($50.00 + $7.50 + est. tax $3.00 = $60.50), and home to payment step in 5 clicks.
  • Marketing box pre-ticked. "Email me with news and offers" is already ticked at checkout, and again on the Shopify sign-in page that the Reward widget opens. A shopper who does not look closely signs up without meaning to. Fix: leave it unticked.
  • Not tested: payment, order confirmation and the confirmation email (no test card).

7. Join

  • Every "join" button leads to login. "Join Today" and the footer "SIGN UP TODAY" open the login page, so the shopper needs an extra click to find "Sign Up" (C2, C16). Fix: send them to /register.
  • Newsletter signup is broken on the club site. The footer button goes to login, the mobile sidebar button throws a script error and opens nothing, and /newsletter is blank (H-6). A shopper who only wants news cannot sign up. Fix: one working email form, used in all three places.
  • The shop newsletter asks for too much. It requires first and last name, email, mobile phone, interests and consent to texts and autodialed calls (Mitten Extracts and AIQ). Many shoppers stop at the phone field. Fix: email-only signup, with SMS as a separate opt-in.
  • Shop and club are two separate accounts. The shop's Reward widget greets you with "WELCOME TO Mitten Club", but Join and Sign in open the Shopify customer login and show no points (H-3). A member cannot see their balance while shopping, and a new shopper may create the wrong account. Fix: connect the widget to the Mitten Club account (single sign-on or account linking) and show the points balance.
  • Registration surprises. Phone is required but is not marked; "Phone is required" only appears after you leave it empty or press Register, digits are not formatted as you type, and the terms box is already ticked. Fix: mark required fields, format the phone number (or make it optional), and leave consent for the user to tick.

8. Verify

  • The code step feels unfinished. After registering, a code goes to both email and phone while the URL stays on /register; resend is locked behind a countdown; the email subject says "one time code to login" even though you are signing up; the "Welcome to Mitten" email arrives before you have entered the code; and after entering it there is no confirmation. You land on the home page looking logged out. A shopper will wonder whether it worked. Fix: email subject "Your Mitten Club sign-up code", send the welcome email after verification, and after the code show "You're in" and go straight to the rewards page, logged in.
  • Product verification hides its rule. On /verify the "exactly 10 characters" rule appears only after you leave the field, and VERIFY stays greyed out without saying why. Product scans are the repeatable way to earn points (the scan campaigns), so members will hit this screen again and again. Fix: helper text with an example and where to find the code on the pack, a character counter, and a button that turns on at 10 characters. A real verification was not tested (no product code available).

9. Rewards

  • A cold start. A new member sees 0 points and no sign-up bonus. Tier (BRONZE, 1000 pts to Silver) appears only on the account page, not on Rewards. Fix: a small welcome bonus, and tier plus progress shown on the rewards page.
  • Member pages that are broken. Collectibles shows only the words "trending" and "product lines" (H-5), and /campaign shows "Sorry We Detected An Issue" (H-4). Both are dead ends for an engaged member. Fix: fix them, or hide them until they have content.

10. Account

  • What works. Profile data is correct, logout is one click, and login with email and password works without a code.
  • No communication preferences. The profile has no newsletter or marketing setting, so a member cannot opt in or out. Fix: email and SMS toggles on Details.
  • Placeholder page title. The reset page's browser tab reads "Reset password | brand name" (L-9). Fix: fill in the brand name.
  • Reset links can be reused and never expire (security, SEC-2). A used reset link still opens the set-password form and accepts a new password (re-verified on Sep 27), and the token has no expiry. This is a risk to members' accounts rather than a speed issue, but it belongs at the top of the developer list.

Top 5 fixes, ranked by impact on a shopper

  1. Add working search to shopmitten.com. Search box in the header, a real results page and a "no results" state, replacing the Lorem Ipsum template.
  2. Make joining one straight path. Every Join or Sign up button goes to /register, the program is explained on the first screen, phone is marked as required (or made optional), and after the code the member sees "You're in" and lands logged in on Rewards.
  3. Connect the shop to the club account. The shop's Reward widget signs in to the Mitten Club account and shows the points balance, instead of opening the Shopify customer login.
  4. Make product verification easy to find and use. Wire the header "Validate" button to /verify, use one name for it, and show the 10-character rule with an example before the member types.
  5. Remove dead ends. Fix or hide /campaign (error page), /collectibles (empty), the club newsletter (goes to login, script error, blank page) and the store-page SHOP NOW button (error toast).
